Operational Rigor

Kitchen Management & Standards

Culinary artistry requires disciplined operations. Here is how I maintain safety, consistency, and peak brigade performance.

HACCP & Food Safety

Rigorous adherence to temperature logs, FIFO inventory rotation, allergen separation protocols, and immaculate sanitation standards across all prep stations.

  • Daily temperature and cooling logs
  • Strict cross-contamination prevention
  • Allergen matrix compliance

Inventory & Waste Control

Optimizing yield management and utilizing root-to-stem culinary techniques to minimize food waste while maximizing ingredient profitability.

  • Weekly stocktaking & variance audits
  • Recipe costing & yield tracking
  • Sustainable trimmings utilization

Brigade Leadership

Mentoring line cooks, delegating station responsibilities efficiently, and maintaining calm, focused communication during peak service rushes.

  • Pre-service briefings & tastings
  • Skill development & knife training
  • Calm, authoritative pass management
